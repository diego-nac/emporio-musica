# Payment options

English translation of an excerpt from an actual demo conversation.

**Customer:** What is the PIX discount?

**Assistant:** PIX payments receive a 5% discount on the list price.

**Customer:** How many installments are available?

**Assistant:** Credit card purchases can be paid in up to 12 interest-free installments. Up to 3 installments have no minimum installment amount except for purchases below R$ 50,00; 4 to 6 installments require a minimum of R$ 80,00 each; and 7 to 12 installments require a minimum of R$ 100,00 each.
