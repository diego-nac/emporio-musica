# Returns and warranty

English translation of an excerpt from an actual demo conversation.

**Customer:** I bought a defective instrument.

**Assistant:** I am sorry your instrument has a problem. Under the store's policy, products with manufacturing defects may be exchanged within 30 calendar days after purchase. After that, within the 90-day legal warranty period, the manufacturer can handle the warranty and the store can help mediate the process. The warranty does not cover misuse, falls, or unauthorized modifications. Please contact the store's customer service with your order number or purchase details for guidance.

**Customer:** What is the return deadline for an online purchase?

**Assistant:** For online purchases, you can request a return within 7 calendar days after receiving the product, without giving a reason. The product must be in its original packaging, with no signs of use and with all accessories and manuals.
